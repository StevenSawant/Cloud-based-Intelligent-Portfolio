========================================================
   PORTFOLIO AI TESTING EXAMPLES
========================================================

Below are test messages you can copy and paste into your 
portfolio's contact form. Each one is designed to trigger
a completely different personalized email response!

--------------------------------------------------------
TEST 1: The "CV / Resume" Trigger
--------------------------------------------------------
COPY THIS:
"Hi Steven, I came across your portfolio and I'm very impressed with your background. Could you please send me your latest CV so I can share it with my team?"

EXPECTED EMAIL INCLUDES:
"I see that you mentioned my C.V in your message. I am happy to forward you my C.V in response..."

--------------------------------------------------------
TEST 2: The "Project / GitHub" Trigger
--------------------------------------------------------
COPY THIS:
"Hello, I enjoyed looking at the projects on this site. I'd love to see more of your code. Do you have a GitHub profile I can review?"

EXPECTED EMAIL INCLUDES:
"The projects I listed on my site only include those not running in production. I have several other projects that might interest you..."

--------------------------------------------------------
TEST 3: The "Article / Blog" Trigger
--------------------------------------------------------
COPY THIS:
"Hi Steven, I really enjoyed reading that data science article you wrote. Do you have a blog setup where I can follow your future writings?"

EXPECTED EMAIL INCLUDES:
"In your message you mentioned my blog posts and data science articles. I have several other articles published in academic journals..."

--------------------------------------------------------
TEST 4: The "Everything Machine" (Triggers all 3!)
--------------------------------------------------------
COPY THIS:
"Hello! Your resume looks fantastic. I also took a look at your github project and read your latest post. Very impressive work overall."

EXPECTED EMAIL INCLUDES:
A massive email combining the CV paragraph, the Project paragraph, and the Article paragraph all into one!

--------------------------------------------------------
TEST 5: Generic Positive (No trigger words used)
--------------------------------------------------------
COPY THIS:
"Hey Steven! Just wanted to reach out and say your website design is absolutely beautiful. Great job!"

EXPECTED EMAIL INCLUDES:
"Thank you for your very kind words about my work! If there is anything else I can assist you with please let me know..."

--------------------------------------------------------
TEST 6: The "Angry User" / Negative Trigger
--------------------------------------------------------
(Note: To test this, you must have the full AWS Comprehend AI enabled. If you are using the MOCK code, this will not work as MOCK is hardcoded to "POSITIVE")

COPY THIS:
"This website is completely broken and the colors are terrible. I am very unhappy with this experience."

EXPECTED EMAIL INCLUDES:
"I see that you are unhappy in your response. Can we please set up a session to discuss why you are not happy..."
